Frequently asked questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

What types of roles do you support?

We focus on multilingual and language-sensitive roles, including customer service, sales, operations and specialist positions. The exact scope is agreed for each hiring brief.

Can you assess candidates we have already sourced?

Yes. Subject to availability and an agreed scope, we can provide language and role-based assessment support for your existing candidate pool.

Do you guarantee a successful hire?

No recruitment process can responsibly guarantee a hire. We agree on the screening process, deliverables, timelines and any replacement terms in writing before work begins.

Can candidates pay to access job opportunities?

Our intended model is employer-paid recruitment and assessment. Candidates should be cautious of anyone claiming that payment guarantees employment.

Do you provide official language certification?

Any internal assessment is a recruitment assessment, not an official international language certificate unless delivered under an authorised certification arrangement.

How do we get started?

Send us your hiring requirements using the contact form. We will review the brief and discuss the most appropriate scope, timeline and commercial terms.
